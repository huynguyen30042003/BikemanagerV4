# Design Taste Frontend (TasteSkill) Directives

Always adhere to the instructions defined in `.agents/skills/design-taste-frontend/SKILL.md`:
1. **Design Read**: Always evaluate the brief and determine the page kind, audience, vibe, and dialed metrics (`DESIGN_VARIANCE`, `MOTION_INTENSITY`, `VISUAL_DENSITY`).
2. **Anti-Slop Standards**: Avoid generic AI templates, purple gradient slop, or repetitive card layouts. Ensure high visual quality, typographic hierarchy, intentional palettes, and strict contrast standards.
3. **Component & Motion Integrity**: Use Motion (`motion/react`) for smooth physics and interactions. Ensure loading, empty, and active tactile states are fully implemented.
4. **Consistency**: Lock typography, border-radius scale, and accent colors across the page.
